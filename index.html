How AI Smart Cleaner Works

A transparent, streamlined 3-step civic workflow connecting citizens and sanitation teams.

01

1. Spot & Report Waste

Take a picture of the unattended garbage, overflowing bin, or blocked drain. Submit with your location in seconds. A unique, secure tracking ID is immediately issued.

02

2. 12-Hour Field Dispatch

The system assigns the nearest active sanitation worker. The team arrives on site, starts operations, and works toward our strict 12-hour resolution deadline.

03

3. Evidence Verification

Field staff upload photographic evidence of the clean spot. Administrators audit and approve the result before marking the complaint verified and publicly visible.
